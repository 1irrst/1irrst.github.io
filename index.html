Mine Deeper

How deep can YOU dig?

Grab your pickaxe and smash your way down through an endless mine full of ores, explosions, treasure and giant bosses. Every swing breaks a block, every block pays Ore Scraps, and every run takes you deeper than the last!

What's waiting down there

  • 12 depth zones: from the sunny Topsoil to the Crystal Caves, the burning Magma, the dark Abyss and the mysterious Aether Rift
  • 9 giant bosses: each one fights its own way. Learn its secret and smash it for huge loot
  • 9 boss abilities: beat a boss and claim its power
  • 16 pickaxes to forge: from Wooden to Aether, each with its own powers
  • Chain reactions: light TNT and watch the mine fall apart
  • Legendary trails: open chests and collect dazzling pickaxe trails
  • Quests every hour: complete them for gems

One finger to play. Easy to pick up, hard to put down.
